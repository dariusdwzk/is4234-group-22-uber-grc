## Simplifications to cite in the report

- `PDPA_26D` starts at `high_since` as a proxy for the notifiable assessment.
- `SEC_1_05` has no holiday calendar: business days are Mon-Fri in UTC.
- Spec §8 assumptions held; `v1.json` labels needed no change. Before relying on the SEC and PDPA rows in the report, read Item 1.05 and s 26D on the primary sites.
